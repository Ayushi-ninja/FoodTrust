AI Freshness Score

The AI algorithms analyze time-since-preparation, ambient temperature, packaging conditions, and food category decay curves to compute a dynamic freshness index (0–100).

  • 90–100 (Fresh): Recently cooked or freshly harvested.
  • 70–89 (Moderate): Safe for standard consumption within window.
  • Below 70 (Critical): Nearing shelf-life limit; immediate priority pickup recommended.
